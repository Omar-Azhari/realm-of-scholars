# Phase 17 — Final Playthrough Report

Played three passes — first hour, mid-semester (partial progress), and
completion — trying to stay in the world. Notes on delight, confusion,
pacing, immersion, and friction.

## First hour (fresh save)

- **Delight**: the 3-step guide, then a quiet campus where the mentors greet
  you by *personality* — Cass (Cloud) deadpans "It works on my machine.
  Fortunately, my machine is the cloud," while Iris (UI) offers "Coffee, then
  wireframes. That's the order." First impression is a *place with people*,
  not a dashboard.
- **Immersion**: objects glow faintly (invite interaction), the prompt names
  the act ("E — Browse Books"), and the mentor points you at real objects
  ("The bookshelf has today's reading: …"). You learn the room by walking it.
- **Pacing**: door → mentor → board/desk is a tight, legible loop. Nothing
  rushes you; the single objective bar answers "what next" without a menu.
- **Friction**: the only unavoidable panel in the first hour is confirming an
  assessment's date (data entry) — reached naturally from the board. Marking
  a module complete is still a tap in Tasks/Subjects (a record act, not a
  physical one) — acceptable, and never forced during exploration.

## Mid-semester (several modules done, some study logged)

- **Delight**: the rooms are now visibly *lived-in* — notes have filled the
  board, a book stack or two has appeared, finished work is piling by the
  desk, the progress whiteboard is half-filled, and after 5pm the desk lamp
  glows and gently flickers. Progress is legible **without opening a panel**.
- **Immersion**: mentors shift register with your state — a real 5-day gap
  earns a warm "There you are! The studio kept your seat warm" instead of a
  scold; a heavy workload earns Mara's "Sustainable pace beats heroics. Take
  a recovery day." The world reacts to *you*.
- **Pacing**: workload-driven mentor tone (busy vs relaxed) makes the middle
  of the semester feel like it has weather. Good rhythm.
- **Friction**: none new. The objective + minimap keep navigation obvious.

## Completion (100%)

- **Delight**: entering a completed subject, the mentor delivers a genuinely
  proud complete-line ("Full gallery, wall to wall. You built a body of
  work.") and the room is at its fullest — max notes, books, filled board.
  The HQ completion sequence (Phase 11) still gathers all four mentors.
- **Immersion**: at high relationship levels mentors switch to milestone
  lines ("I'd invest in you. We've built real traction together in here."),
  which lands emotionally after a semester.
- **Pacing/friction**: completion feels earned, not abrupt; nothing locks;
  you can keep wandering.

## Summary

The explore → talk → study loop runs menu-light across the whole semester,
and the world now carries the personality and atmosphere the phase targeted:
distinct mentors, rooms that fill with your work, and small ambient life. The
remaining friction points (date confirmation, module-complete tap) are record
actions with no physical equivalent and are correctly left as quick shortcuts,
never blockers.
