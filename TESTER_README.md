# Welcome to the UniGame playtest! 🎓

UniGame turns a real university semester into a small living campus. Four
subject rooms, a mentor in each, a study timer that rewards real focus, and
a semester that visibly moves around you. You're testing **v1.0.0-rc.1**.

## Getting in (no installation)

1. Open the playtest URL your facilitator sent you. That's it — it's a web
   app; nothing installs.
2. **Use a normal browser window, not private/incognito** — private windows
   delete your progress when they close.
3. Optional: on a phone you can "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like icon.

Your progress lives **only in your browser** on that device. Nothing is
uploaded anywhere.

## Recommended browsers

- **Best tested:** Chrome or Edge (desktop & Android), any recent version.
- **Works, less tested:** Firefox.
- **Safari / iPhone:** should work, but you are our first real Safari
  testers — Safari bug reports are extra valuable! Mention Safari + iOS
  version in any report.
- Anything newer than early 2023 is fine; very old browsers may lose some
  colour effects (harmless).

## Expected play time

- First session: **25–35 minutes** exploring + one real Focus Sprint.
- Then, ideally, **~10 minutes on 2–3 later days** — streaks, reminders,
  and the "campus feels alive" effect only show up across days.

## How to report bugs

Send whatever channel your facilitator gave you (chat/email/form), with:

1. **What you did → what happened → what you expected.**
2. The version line from **Settings → About** (e.g. "UniGame v1.0.0-rc.1 ·
   build a1b2c3").
3. Browser + device (e.g. "Safari, iPhone 15, iOS 18").
4. A screenshot if it's visual.
5. For progress-related bugs, attach your save: **Settings → Data → Export
   save data (.json)** — it lets us reproduce your exact game state.

Console errors (if you know how to open DevTools) are gold, but never
required.

## Backing up your progress

Settings → Data → **Export save data** downloads a `.json` backup any time.
Do it after long sessions. **Import** restores it — including on a
different device or browser. Full details: BACKUP_GUIDE.md.

## Known limitations (please don't report these)

- **All characters and rooms are placeholder art** — final art arrives
  later without affecting your progress.
- **There is no sound yet.** The Settings toggle exists for a future update.
- Week numbers appear only after you confirm your semester start date on
  the Calendar screen (we never guess dates).
- Mentor bond level 5 and end-of-semester visuals are semester-scale goals —
  you won't reach them during the playtest, and that's expected.
- "Needs confirmation" badges on dates/weights are intentional: those
  details weren't in our source documents, so *you* confirm them once and
  the app treats your value as truth.
